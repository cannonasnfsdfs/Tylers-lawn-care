A Little About Tyler

Tyler's love for lawn care started long before he ever thought about turning it into a business.

When Tyler was a kid, he would walk around the neighborhood looking for lawns that needed mowing. He wasn't trying to build a company back then. He just wanted to make a little money.

Tyler remembers asking neighbors if he could cut their grass for a dollar. After finishing the job, he'd head straight to the store with his money to buy himself some gum.

“It started with a dollar, a lawn mower, and a kid who really wanted some gum.”

Years later, that same work ethic is still what Tyler brings to every lawn. What started as a kid trying to earn a dollar turned into a passion for making yards look their best.
